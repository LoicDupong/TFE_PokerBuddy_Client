## Design Context

### Users
Groups of friends who organise home poker nights.
- Players use PokerBuddy mostly on their phone, before the night (answer an invite, check time and place, join via link) and after it (results, stats, friends leaderboard).
- The host also uses it during the game: the Game Manager (timer, blinds, level) is projected on a TV, a computer screen or a tablet so every player at the table can read it from a distance, often in dim lighting.
- Jobs to be done: create a game and get friends to answer quickly; run the night without a phone timer and paper notes; settle results and bragging rights afterwards.

### Brand Personality
- Tone: "entre potes, fun". Relaxed, a bit cheeky, the banter of a poker night among friends. Never corporate, never casino-luxury.
- Three words: convivial, cheeky, readable.
- Emotional goals: excitement before the night, zero friction at the table, pride (or teasing) after the results.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Keep the existing identity: PokerBuddy logo, dark navy background (#1a1b26), red accent (#d70022), light periwinkle (#9fa5df), off-white text (#fff3f3). Dark theme stays (evening use, TV projection).
- Typography may evolve (currently Roboto everywhere).
- Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboard, online-casino neon, corporate admin panel.

### Design Principles
1. Phone first for players: every player action (answer an invite, check a game, see results) works in one or two taps on a small screen.
2. Readable from across the table: the Game Manager is designed for a TV or tablet seen from 2-3 metres in a dim room.
3. Status at a glance: who is coming, what is pending, who won must be visible without reading paragraphs.
4. Friendly copy: short, warm, poker vocabulary used naturally, a touch of humour where it does not slow the user down.
5. Keep the identity, raise the craft: improve hierarchy, spacing and feedback within the existing colours and logo.
